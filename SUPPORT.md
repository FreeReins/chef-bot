# Getting help with ChefBot

1. **Read the docs.** [Troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting/index.md) lists common problems and their fixes, and the
   [FAQ](docs/reference/faq.md) answers common questions. The same pages are on the
   [documentation site](https://jamiethenextkevinbaconhamm.github.io/chef-bot/) and inside ChefBot under **?**.
2. **Search the issues.** Someone may have had the same problem:
   [open and closed issues](https://github.com/FreeReins/chef-bot/issues?q=is%3Aissue).
3. **Open an issue.** [Choose a template](https://github.com/FreeReins/chef-bot/issues/new/choose),
   and include your version, how you installed ChefBot, and the log.

Security problems are reported privately instead: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
