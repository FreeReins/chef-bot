# Cooking mode

Cooking mode shows a recipe in large text, one step at a time, so you can follow it from across the kitchen. Open any
recipe and press **Start cooking**.

## What you see

1. **Gather your ingredients:** everything to set out before you start, for the servings chosen on the recipe's
   page. See [Make it for more or fewer people](recipes.md#make-it-for-more-or-fewer-people).
2. **Before you start:** anything that takes a while and is needed later, such as preheating the oven, a pot of
   water, or an overnight marinade. ChefBot finds these in the steps for you. Steps that wait a long time are
   flagged, so you can read ahead and dinner isn't late.
3. **One step at a time,** with the ingredients that step uses. Press **Next** and **Back** to move between steps,
   and **Finish** at the end.

## Timers

Times written in a step, such as "simmer for 20 minutes", become timers. Start one with a tap. It rings when it ends,
and several can run at once.

## Handy details

- **Larger text:** make the text bigger with the button at the top.
- **The screen stays on** while cooking mode is open, in browsers that allow it.
- **Leave cooking mode** with the button at the top, or your browser's back button.

When you're done, go back to the Plan tab and mark the meal **Cooked**, so its ingredients come out of the pantry.
