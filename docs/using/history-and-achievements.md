# History and achievements

ChefBot remembers what your household cooks. The **History** tab tells it as a story, and **Achievements** turns it
into ranks and badges. Neither changes how meals are planned. They're there for fun, and to remind you of the
recipes you loved.

## History

The **History** tab shows your kitchen's story month by month, newest first:

- recipes added and retired;
- dinners cooked, with debuts, fan favourites, comebacks and milestone dinners picked out, and when someone's
  favourite was cooked;
- reviews, with their stars and notes;
- big shopping hauls;
- people joining the household.

It shows six months at a time. Press **Dig deeper** for older months.

## Achievements

Open **Achievements** from the award in the header. It shows:

- **Your rank,** from 0 to 5, and the points to the next one. Points come from badges.
- **Starter steps:** add a recipe, plan a week, confirm a plan, buy a whole shopping list, cook a planned meal, and
  make a recipe a favourite. Inviting someone is an optional step and earns no points, so a household of one can
  reach every rank.
- **Badges,** each with tiers to reach: recipes in the library, dinners cooked, different recipes cooked, weeks
  planned, whole shopping lists bought, the longest run of weeks with something cooked, comebacks, reviews written
  and favourites marked.

Nothing here ever goes down. An admin can have new achievements sent as a notification. See
[Notifications](../admin/notifications.md).
