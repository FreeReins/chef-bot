# Glossary

Words ChefBot uses, in plain terms.

- **Admin**: An account that can invite people, reset passwords and change household-wide settings. The first account is the admin.
- **Course**: What kind of recipe it is: main, breakfast, snack, sweet, drink, or not a meal.
- **Confirmed plan**: A plan you've accepted. Its shopping list is the one to shop from, and it appears in the calendar feed.
- **Draft** (staged plan): A plan to review, swap and shuffle before confirming. Nothing has been bought against it.
- **Favourite**: A recipe someone has hearted. Plan styles can prefer favourites.
- **Ingredient catalog**: ChefBot's list of known ingredients, with each one's food group, shelf life and season.
- **Leftover lunch**: A weekday lunch planned from the leftovers of a recent dinner. That dinner is cooked big enough for it.
- **MCP**: Model Context Protocol: the way AI assistants such as Claude connect to ChefBot.
- **Not a meal**: Recipes such as sides and sauces, which are never planned on their own.
- **Pantry**: The food you have at home, with expiry dates.
- **Perishable**: An ingredient that keeps for a short time (21 days or less to start with). Plans try to use it in more than one meal.
- **Plan style**: What a week holds: the days you cook dinner, leftover lunches, how many breakfasts, snacks, sweets and drinks, and rules for the recipes.
- **Replan from pantry**: Plans a week again, built around what's already in the pantry.
- **Rule**: Part of a plan style, such as "at least 2 dinners ready within 30 minutes".
- **Staple**: An ingredient you always have, such as salt or oil, left off the shopping list.
- **Webhook**: An address ChefBot posts reminders to, such as a Home Assistant automation.
